THE PROBLEM

If the water doesn't warm up, everything downstream works harder.

A district cooling plant sells the temperature gap between supply and return water, not the water itself. Low Delta-T Syndrome means that gap collapses — forcing far more pumping to deliver the same cooling, and in Tower E's case, pushing right up against installed pump capacity.

THE PRODUCT

Three layers, each built from a specific finding

Not a generic monitoring dashboard — every layer exists because a root cause demanded it.

01 — SENSE

Dual-feedback sensing

Digital + analog valve position feedback, clamp-on secondary-loop flow and Delta-T sensors, and sensor/comms upkeep built into the spec.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Load-normalized diagnosis

A regression baseline judges Delta-T against actual load and weather, and a fault-signature classifier names bypass, fouling or valve fault by shape.

03 — ACT

Self-scheduling maintenance

Rolling trend detection on valve drift, fouling rate and pump-capacity headroom auto-generates a work order — with a predicted date, before a fault fires.
